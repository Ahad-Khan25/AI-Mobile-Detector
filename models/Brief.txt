Contains all ML models, their checkpoints, and training outputs.
models->phone_detector: Stores YOLO or custom models trained to detect mobile phones.
models->student_detector: Stores models responsible for identifying and tracking students within the classroom.
models->hand_model: Contains models used for hand landmark detection or hand pose tracking.
models->head_pose: Stores models for head pose estimation(yaw, pitch, roll detection)
models->training: Contains training scripts, optimization pipelines, and model export utilities. Used only during development, not runtime.

Training will happen in models->training here we will keep Jupyter notebooks, Training scripts, Model optimization
scripts, Experiment tracking, Training logs, Hyperparameter configurations.
After training each model is saved into its OWN directory which will contain the final versions of models i.e.
.py files, .onnx(Open Neural Network format) files that allow AI models to run anywhere, config YAML files,
version metadata
Then the .py files use the final models.
